# Watch parties

Watch with friends, in sync: anything one of you plays opens for everyone. Pause, skip or go back and it happens for all of you, and the party stays together from one episode to the next.

## Start a party

1. On Home, press **Triangle** (or click your profile) and choose **Start a watch party**.
2. Choose **Start the party**.
3. Press **Play** on anything: it opens for everyone in the party.

Friends on your server get an **invite** on their Home. They press **Triangle** (or click **Join**) to come in. If a party is already going, you'll see it on the Start screen too, so you can join it instead.

## While in a party

- A pill at the top right of Home shows who's in it. Open it for **Join what everyone is watching** and **Leave party**.
- Everyone can play, pause and skip, and keeps their own subtitles and audio.
- You only leave when you choose **Leave party**, switch profile or sign out.

## What the server needs

In Jellyfin, every profile needs **SyncPlay access**: **Dashboard → Users → *profile* → SyncPlay access**.

Frame's instant sync mode also needs **Allow remote control of other users** for each profile that uses it.

::: info Official Jellyfin apps
Parties work with the official Jellyfin apps too, but those can cause their own sync loops. Frame-to-Frame parties are the smoothest.
:::
