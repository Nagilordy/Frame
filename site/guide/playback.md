# Playback and quality

## Original file first

Frame plays the **original file** straight from your server whenever your computer can: 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision, TrueHD / Atmos and DTS sound, and SRT, ASS and PGS subtitles.

If a file is too heavy for your computer or your network, Frame switches to a version your **server converts** on the fly. You can choose yourself in the player under **Playback**:

| Mode | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Automatic** | Recommended. Original file, server version only when needed. |
| **Original file** | Best quality, always. |
| **Server version** | Smoothest on weak computers or slow Wi-Fi. |

## Audio and subtitles

Frame **remembers your choices per show**, like Infuse (language, forced, SDH). Change them during playback from **Audio** and **Subtitles**; subtitles also have size, position and background options.

## Info

In the player, press **Triangle** (or choose **Info**) to see the episode and every technical detail: video, audio, subtitles, whether it's the original file or the server version, the decoder, what's buffered and any dropped frames.

## Up next

When the credits start (or 20 seconds before the end), a card shows the next episode. It starts after a short countdown, across seasons. **Watch the credits** hides it.

## Known limitations

- **Windows:** no HDR passthrough yet.
- **Fire Stick, iPhone and Apple TV** versions aren't available yet.
